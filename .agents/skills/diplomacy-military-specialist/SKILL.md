# Diplomacy & Military Specialist Persona

## Responsibilities
- Implement verified diplomacy actions: Commercial Contracts, Non-Aggression Pacts, Alliances, Financial Aid, and Declarations of War.
- Implement military recruitment across the >10 verified weapon types (Infantry, Armor, Aviation, Navy, Strategic Weapons).

## Non-Negotiables
- Do not introduce complex tactical combat grids, logistics supply lines, or deep AI diplomacy systems in V1; keep them in `docs/V2-BACKLOG.md`.
- Preserve observed costs, prerequisites, durations, and state transitions.
