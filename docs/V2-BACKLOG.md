# V2 Backlog: Post-Parity Enhancements & Advanced Simulation Roadmap

- **Product:** Rashtra: Sovereign Republic
- **Status:** Isolated V2 Roadmap (Quarantined from V1 Parity Scope)
- **Gate Policy:** Features in this document are strictly deferred until after the V1.0.0 release is signed off.

---

## 1. Domain 1: Sub-National & Federal State Administration
- **India State & Union Territory Layer:**
  - 28 States and 8 Union Territories with individual Chief Ministers, state legislative assemblies, and regional public approval metrics.
  - State-level fiscal autonomy: State Goods & Services Tax (SGST), central revenue devolutions via Finance Commission formulas.
  - Inter-state development dynamics: Regional infrastructure disparity, river water disputes, inter-state migrant labor policies.
- **Governor Appointments & President's Rule:**
  - Emergency constitutional mechanisms to invoke central administration during severe civil unrest or breakdown of state law and order.

---

## 2. Domain 2: Macroeconomic & Demographic Depth
- **Dynamic Inflation & Monetary Policy:**
  - Reserve Bank / Central Bank benchmark repo rate adjustments affecting commercial bank lending, consumer inflation, and capital flight.
  - Foreign exchange reserves (Forex) tracking liquid USD, Euro, Gold, and Special Drawing Rights (SDR).
- **Demographic & Labor Market Modeling:**
  - Age demographic pyramid (Youth dividend vs aging populations), labor force participation rates, skill development training programs.
  - Brain-drain mitigation initiatives, higher education research grants, rural-to-urban migration pressure on urban infrastructure.
- **Detailed Commodity Supply Chains:**
  - Intermediate industrial goods (Semiconductors, Rare Earths, Lithium, Petrochemicals, Steel billets) with trade route dependency and port congestion.

---

## 3. Domain 3: Autonomous Geopolitical AI & Multilateral Diplomacy
- **Strategic AI Personalities:**
  - Goal-driven national archetypes (Revanchist, Isolationist, Hegemonic, Mercantile, Non-Aligned) operating with multi-turn diplomatic plans.
- **Multilateral International Organizations:**
  - United Nations General Assembly & Security Council resolutions, international peacekeeping deployments, binding international sanctions.
  - Multilateral blocs (G20, BRICS, Quad, SCO, ASEAN) with collective security treaties and preferential trade agreements.
- **Sovereign Debt Bond Markets:**
  - Issuing sovereign bonds with market-determined yields, credit rating agency reviews (Moody's, S&P, Fitch analogues).

---

## 4. Domain 4: Military Logistics, Defense Industry & Tactical Combat
- **Defense Indigenization & Procurement:**
  - Domestic research & development programs (Make in India defense corridors) reducing reliance on foreign weapon platforms.
  - Munitions stockpiles, fuel reserves, and supply depot networks dictating operational combat endurance.
- **Theater-Level Naval & Aerial Operations:**
  - Sea-lane protection, naval blockades, submarine deterrence patrols, and air superiority corridors.
- **Optional 3D Tactical Combat Visualizer:**
  - Stylized low-poly tactical overview for major decisive engagements, displaying unit maneuvering, terrain modifiers, and air support strikes.

---

## 5. Domain 5: Modding Ecosystem & Scenario Editor
- **In-Engine Scenario Editor:**
  - Visual interface for authoring custom starting scenarios (e.g., Cold War 1971, Multipolar 2035, Alternate History).
  - Custom nation creator with flag editor, border vector drawing tools, and starting asset distributions.
- **Steam Workshop / Local Mod Loader:**
  - Modular JSON pack loading for community translations, custom unit rosters, historical event packs, and user interface themes.
