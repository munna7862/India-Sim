# V2 Backlog: Improvements Deferred Until V1 Parity Release

This backlog is intentionally separate from V1 sprint work. Prioritise only after the V1 parity gate is accepted.

Potential V2 candidates:
- Detailed India state/UT budgets and public services.
- Deeper macroeconomics, population, labour and resource production.
- More autonomous country AI and long-horizon national goals.
- More sophisticated diplomacy, international organisations and crisis systems.
- Infrastructure, logistics, trade route capacity and supply chains.
- More detailed military readiness and strategic logistics.
- Richer map overlays, historical trends and explanatory dashboards.
- Additional 3D city/infrastructure views if they materially improve the experience.
- Modding and scenario editor.

Do not implement these during V1 unless the human owner explicitly changes scope.
